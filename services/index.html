01 — Design

Architectural Design

Concept · Schematic · Detailing · Permit drawings

Every project begins with the brief — your life, your business, the realities of the site. We translate that into floor plans, massing studies, material palettes, and detail drawings that earn their place in construction. Our drawings are produced for builders, not for portfolios — so what's drawn is what's built.

We work in 3D from day one and present concepts with photoreal renderings so you can feel the space before we break ground.

  • Concept & massing studies
  • 3D renderings & walkthroughs
  • Schematic & design development
  • Construction documents
  • Permit submission & approval
  • Material & finish specifications
02 — Residential

Luxury Home & Villa Construction

Custom homes · Modern villas · Apartment buildings

Custom homes and villas across Addis Ababa — Bole, Sarbet, CMC, Old Airport, Megenagna, Ayat, Summit, Lebu. We build from a clean slate or remodel an existing structure. Stone, concrete, glass, hardwood — we work in the materials that make Ethiopian luxury distinctive.

Apartment building projects benefit from the same discipline: a single architectural vision, executed by a contractor who's responsible to it.

  • Custom luxury villas
  • Single-family modern homes
  • High-end apartment buildings
  • Whole-home renovations
  • Outdoor & landscape integration
  • Smart-home pre-wiring
03 — Commercial

Commercial & Office Remodeling

Office · Retail · Hospitality · Mixed-use

Strategic commercial remodels in Addis Ababa — offices, retail, restaurants, boutique hospitality. We transform existing space to elevate brand, operations, and resale value, without losing weeks of revenue to a slow build.

  • Office & co-working fit-out
  • Retail flagship build-outs
  • Restaurant & café design
  • Hotel & guesthouse remodels
  • Mixed-use & mid-rise builds
  • Brand-aligned interiors
04 — Interiors

Interior Design & Fit-Out

Curated palettes · Custom millwork · Lighting design

Interior design that's structurally aware. Because we build what we draw, our interior plans account for HVAC, electrical, and plumbing realities from day one — meaning fewer site changes and tighter execution.

We curate material palettes, specify lighting, source furniture, and through our sister company 2:20 Woodworks, we build cabinetry and millwork to the architect's drawings.

  • Material & finish curation
  • Custom millwork & cabinetry
  • Lighting design
  • FF&E specification & sourcing
  • Bathroom & kitchen design
  • Stone, wood & metal detailing
05 — Build & Manage

Construction Supervision & Project Management

Daily site supervision · Budget control · Schedule

Beniam Tilahun and our site team supervise every project daily. Budgets are tracked weekly, schedules are real, and the site is run with discipline — clean, organized, and accountable. When the architect's drawing leaves the studio, it survives the journey to the site.

  • Pre-construction planning
  • Daily site supervision
  • Budget & cost reporting
  • Subcontractor coordination
  • Quality assurance & QC
  • Permit & inspection management
