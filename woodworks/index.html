— The Workshop

Built in-house.
Built to last.

Most builders outsource their cabinetry. We don't.

2:20 Woodworks is our in-house millwork shop — a small, hands-on team of craftsmen who build to architectural specification, with materials and joinery we choose ourselves. Every kitchen, every closet, every vanity that finishes a 2:20 Partners project comes from our own workshop.

We also take on direct commissions for clients who want custom millwork without a full design-build engagement — kitchens, wardrobes, libraries, or one-off pieces of furniture.

— What We Build

Capabilities.

— 01

Kitchen Cabinetry

Full custom kitchens — base and wall cabinets, islands, pantry walls, and integrated appliance surrounds. Dovetail drawers, soft-close hardware, and finishes built for daily use.

— 02

Built-in Closets

Walk-in wardrobes, bedroom closets, and entry built-ins. Designed around your wardrobe, not a catalog. Drawers, hanging zones, shoe walls, and lit interiors.

— 03

Vanities & Sink Units

Bathroom vanities and laundry sink units with integrated stone or wood tops, soft-close drawers, and storage tuned to plumbing and lighting.

— 04

Custom Furniture

One-off pieces — dining tables, headboards, bookcases, desks, and credenzas. Built to fit your space exactly, with materials and proportions chosen for the room.

— 05

Interior Doors

Solid-core interior doors, pivot doors, and concealed-frame doors. Matched to wall finishes and hardware for a seamless architectural look.

— 06

Commercial Millwork

Reception desks, conference tables, retail fixtures, and office built-ins. Production-grade quality with the same craftsmanship as our residential work.

— Why Custom

The case for custom.

— 01

Built for Your Space

No off-the-shelf cabinet ever truly fits. Custom millwork uses every inch — corners, ceiling heights, awkward angles — without compromise.

— 02

Materials That Last

We choose solid hardwoods and premium plywood cores — not the particle board that warps after a few years in Addis humidity.

— 03

Hardware That Works

Soft-close hinges, full-extension drawer slides, and concealed mounting. Details that you'll feel every day for the next twenty years.

— 04

One Team, One Standard

Same partners, same crew, same workshop. Whether it's a full home or a single closet, you get our complete attention.
