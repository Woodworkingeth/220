— What We Do

Our
Services.

From concept to completion, 2:20 Partners delivers full-spectrum design and construction solutions tailored to the demands of Addis Ababa's growing urban landscape. We bridge the gap between visionary architecture and precise construction.

Architectural Design

Custom residential and commercial design — from schematic concept through permit-ready drawings, with a focus on how Addis lives, builds, and ages.

High-End Residential Construction

Boutique home building with clean detailing, honest schedules, and quality standards that hold up over time. Every project led directly by the partners.

Commercial Construction & Remodeling

From new commercial towers to interior fit-outs, we build offices, healthcare, and retail spaces to international standards.

Interior Design & Custom Millwork

Full interior design backed by our in-house woodworking shop — custom cabinetry, closets, vanities, and furniture, all under one roof.

— How We Work

Our Process.

— 01

Discovery & Brief

We begin with a deep listening session — understanding your vision, constraints, timeline, and aspirations for the space.

— 02

Design & Concept

Samson and the design team develop concept designs, space plans, and material palettes that translate your brief into a compelling vision.

— 03

Engineering & Permitting

Detailed structural drawings, MEP coordination, and full permitting handled by our engineering team.

— 04

Construction

Benyam and our crews build with weekly site updates, photo progress, and direct partner oversight on every detail.

— 05

Handover & Care

Final walkthrough, punch-list resolution, warranty, and post-handover support that lasts beyond move-in.
