The Shop

Built where the drawings are made.

2:20 Woodworks operates as the in-house joinery for 2:20 Partners — but takes on independent commissions, too. Because our makers sit one room away from the architects, what's drawn is what's built. Profiles, joinery details, hardware specs, and finish tolerances are agreed before a single board is cut.

We work in Ethiopian hardwoods — Tid, Wanza, Zigba, Tikur Inchet — paired with imported veneers, brass, stone, and machined steel for hardware. Every commission is hand-finished and signed.

What We Make

Six categories.

01

Custom Kitchens

Full kitchens from cabinetry to islands to integrated appliances. Hardwood frames, soft-close hardware, stone or wood counters, brass or steel fixtures — built to the architect's plan, fitted to the millimeter.

02

Walk-In Closets

Dressing rooms designed for how you actually live. Floor-to-ceiling cabinetry, integrated lighting, dressing islands, jewelry drawers, and pull-outs sized to your wardrobe.

03

Vanities & Bathrooms

Wall-hung or freestanding vanities with our signature integrated stone-and-wood sinks. Floating shelves, medicine cabinets, and tall towers — built to match the cabinetry of the rest of the home.

04

Doors & Hardware

Solid hardwood doors — interior, exterior, pocket, pivot — with brass or matte-black hardware. Finished in matching tones to the floor and trim.

05

Built-Ins & Millwork

Library walls, media units, mudroom benches, banquettes, paneling, and trim. The structural carpentry that makes a home feel finished.

06

Statement Furniture

One-off dining tables, beds, desks, sideboards, and seating. Made in Ethiopian hardwoods to a bespoke commission and signed by the maker.

Materials

Local hardwood, imported detail.

We work primarily in Ethiopian hardwoods — selected for grain, durability, and origin. Hardware, veneers, and stone come from a small group of trusted suppliers we've worked with for years.

Tid (Juniper)

Aromatic, tight-grained, naturally rot-resistant. Our default for cabinetry frames and structural millwork.

Wanza

Deep red-brown with dramatic figure. Used for statement furniture and feature panels.

Zigba (Podo)

Light, even-grained, takes finish beautifully. Our choice for closets, vanities, and lighter cabinetry.

Tikur Inchet

Black wood — dense, hard, hand-rubbed to a deep luster. Reserved for accents, hardware, and one-off pieces.

From Brief to Delivery

How a commission works.

01

Brief & Drawing

We start from architect's drawings or a fresh brief. Profiles, materials, and hardware specs are agreed before fabrication.

02

Sample & Sign-Off

For larger commissions we make a finish sample first — wood, stain, hardware — for client sign-off before production.

03

Build & Finish

Cut, joinery, sanding, finishing — all in our shop. Hand-finished by the same maker who started the piece.

04

Install & Tune

We deliver and install on site. Final tuning — alignments, hardware, soft-close adjustment — happens in place.
